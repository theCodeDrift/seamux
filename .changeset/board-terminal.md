Add a terminal to the board: the terminal icon in the header, before Configure, opens one reusable shell on the Mac, in a cmux workspace of its own, so you can tail a log from your phone. It shows the last 500 lines as plain text, read again every second, with a box to type in and keys for Ctrl+C, Esc, Tab, the arrows and Backspace. Closing the panel leaves the shell running; `exit` ends it. Terminal font, in Configure's General tab, sets its font on any device that has that font installed.
