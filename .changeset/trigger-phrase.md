A trigger phrase sends for you: a prompt in the dispatch bar, or a reply in a card or a chat's full view, that you type to end in "make it so" goes on its own after 10 seconds, with the send button turning the colour of a waiting chat and counting down meanwhile, so a dictated message can be sent by saying so. The phrase isn't sent, any edit starts the count over, and taking the phrase off stops it. Change the phrase or the wait, or turn it off, under Trigger phrase in the General tab.
