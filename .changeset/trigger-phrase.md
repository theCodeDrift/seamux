The dispatch bar has a trigger phrase: a prompt that ends in "make it so" dispatches on its own after 7 seconds, with Dispatch counting down meanwhile, so a dictated prompt can be sent by saying so. The phrase isn't sent, any edit starts the count over, and taking the phrase off stops it. Change the phrase or the wait, or turn it off, under Trigger phrase in the General tab.
