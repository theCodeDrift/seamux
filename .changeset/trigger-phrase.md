A trigger phrase sends for you: a prompt in the dispatch bar, or a reply in a card or a chat's full view, that you type to end in "make it so" goes on its own after 15 seconds, with the send button turning the colour of a waiting chat and counting down meanwhile, so a dictated message can be sent by saying so. The phrase isn't sent, any edit starts the count over, and taking the phrase off stops it. Closing a chat's full view while it counts doesn't stop it: the card carries on counting and sends it, and on a phone its Open chat button counts down meanwhile. Change the phrase or the wait, or turn it off, under Trigger phrase in the General tab.
